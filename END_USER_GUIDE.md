---

## 🔧 **Configuration (Required):**

You need to set up these API credentials:

### **Telegram Bot Token:**
1. Message @BotFather on Telegram
2. Type `/newbot` and follow instructions
3. Copy the token (looks like: `1234567890:ABCdefGHI...`)

### **MetaAPI Credentials:**
1. Sign up at https://app.metaapi.cloud/
2. Connect your MT5 trading account
3. Copy your **Token** and **Account ID**

### **Channel Setup:**
1. Add your bot to your trading signals channel
2. Get the channel ID (use @userinfobot)
3. Enter the ID in settings (looks like: `-1001234567890`)
